### 👋 Welcome! Here's what this chatbot can do

I'm an AI assistant that can chat, look things up, and read your documents. Type your question below to get started.

| I can... | How | Try asking |
|---|---|---|
| 💬 **Chat and explain things** | Just ask in plain language | *"Explain how RAG works in simple terms"* |
| 🌐 **Search the web** | Ask about news, weather, or anything recent | *"What's the weather in Mumbai today?"* |
| 📈 **Look up stock prices** | Give a company or ticker (add `.NS` for NSE stocks) | *"Price of AAPL"*, *"Price of RELIANCE.NS"* |
| 🧮 **Do calculations** | Ask for any basic arithmetic | *"What is 12.5% of 840?"* |
| 📄 **Answer from your PDF** | Upload a PDF in the sidebar, then ask about it | *"What does my PDF say about pricing?"* |

**Your chats**

- Every chat is named automatically from your first message and saved. Pick one in the sidebar to continue it, or press **New Chat** to start fresh. To remove a chat for good, click the 🗑️ next to it and confirm.

**Good to know**

- When I use a tool, you'll see a "Using ..." status while it works, and which tool I used afterwards (for example, `rag_tool` means I checked your PDF).
- Stock prices come from Yahoo Finance and can be delayed. Web search can occasionally fail; if it does, just ask again.
- I answer from the most relevant parts of your PDF, so ask about specific topics rather than the whole document. It's one PDF per chat, and scanned (image-only) PDFs can't be read. Your PDF stays with its chat, even after the app restarts, and is removed if you delete the chat.
- Your messages, and text from PDFs you upload, are sent to OpenAI to generate answers. Searches go to DuckDuckGo. I can make mistakes, so double-check anything important.
